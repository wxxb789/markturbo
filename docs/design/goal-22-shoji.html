B — “Shoji” (sliding screens)
Every surface is a toned paper screen sliding on a continuous track; space is rearranged physically, never swapped.
Sidebar: Toned paper panel pushes the stage ≥1040 px and slides over it below; collapses to a 44 px vertical-tick edge strip.
Unified list: Open files mark the tree with an ink dot; outside-folder tabs pin at the top only when present.
View track: One 3-segment glyph & draggable divider slide between Edit (100%), Split (50%), and Preview (0%).
Motion: Interruptible spring-settle ease-out (CAMERA_MS 360 ms, RAIL_WIDEN_MS 160 ms); 8 px slip on clean reload; opacity-only when reduced.
Keys: Ctrl+B sidebar · Ctrl+E view
